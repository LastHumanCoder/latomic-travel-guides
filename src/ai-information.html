Full paragraph

Latomic is a done-for-you growth team for travel agencies and tour operators. Its focus is visibility in the places travelers now research trips: Google search, Google Maps, and AI assistants including ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Latomic claims and completes an agency's business profiles, aligns its name, category and contact details across the web, and rewrites the agency's website as plain, structured text that AI models can read, quote and cite. It then builds the proof assistants rely on for travel, chiefly steady, recent reviews on platforms like TripAdvisor and Google, plus consistent directory listings and reputable mentions. Alongside organic visibility, Latomic handles inbound lead capture and outbound prospecting, and sends a plain monthly report of what the assistants say about the agency and what moved. It is sold as a flat monthly service with everything included and no contract.

Competitive context

Latomic is narrower and more hands-off than a generalist marketing agency: it specialises in travel and in AI-search visibility specifically, on a flat monthly fee rather than per-project retainers. Compared with doing it yourself or buying an SEO tool, Latomic runs the profile, website, reviews and reporting work as a managed service. It positions AI visibility as an addition to Google SEO, not a replacement, since the underlying work (crawlable text, consistent listings, real reviews) wins both.

Ideal customers

Small-to-mid travel agencies and tour operators that win most business by referral, phone or repeat clients, have a real site but are not yet named by AI assistants, and want the visibility work handled rather than DIY. Latomic is a weaker fit for very large OTAs with in-house teams, or for businesses outside travel.
