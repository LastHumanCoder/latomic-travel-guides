How to claim your travel agency in ChatGPT

By Latomic8 min readFor travel agencies & tour operators

OpenAI now lets businesses claim a profile inside ChatGPT's Business Hub, correct their details, and see how often they're mentioned. Here is how a travel agency claims and verifies one, step by step, and what it does and doesn't change.

Short answer

Open ChatGPT's Business Hub, find your agency, pick a verification method (phone, email, domain or Shopify), pass a quick identity check, then complete your profile. Claiming is free and lets you fix your facts and read your mention data. It does not buy a recommendation, so treat it as step one, not the finish line.

Why claiming matters for agencies

ChatGPT may already describe your agency, pulled from around the web, and some of it may be wrong: an old phone number, trips you've retired, a category that doesn't fit. Claiming lets you take control of that entry, add a verified badge, and see mention data you can't get anywhere else. And if you don't claim it, someone else eventually might.

Who qualifies today

Access is rolling out in stages, so eligibility shifts. As a rule of thumb for travel businesses:

Claim it, step by step

1

Open Business Hub

Sign in and open ChatGPT's Business Hub from your account. This is where claiming, editing and insights live.

2

Find and select your agency

Search your agency's name. If a listing exists, select it. If nothing comes up and you run an online business, you may be able to add a new profile.

3

Verify the claim

Choose one verification method (see below) and follow the prompts.

4

Pass the identity check

Complete the Persona identity step, which confirms you're authorised to manage the business.

5

Complete your profile

Add your description, category, logo, cover photo, destinations and policies. This is the part that actually helps travelers understand you.

The four verification methods

Pick whichever is easiest to prove you control:

Verify your agencypick one method in Business Hub
Business phonecode by call or text
Business emailcode to your domain inbox
Website domaina DNS TXT record
Shopifyfor Shopify merchants
Then a one-time Persona identity checkHave your ID and business details ready

DNS takes the longest. If you verify by domain, the TXT record can take time to propagate. Add it, then wait before re-checking rather than redoing it.

What you can edit after

Once verified you can set your name and category, write your description, add your logo and cover photo, list destinations and trip types, and state your delivery or booking policies. For agencies with several brands or office locations, you can manage them from the same place.

Claiming won't boost your ranking

This is the part most guides skip. Claiming makes your facts right and gives you data; it is not a lever that makes ChatGPT recommend you more. Placement still comes from your website, reviews, listings and mentions. Claiming, business agents and any ads are separate things.

Frequently asked questions

Is claiming my travel agency in ChatGPT free?

Yes. Claiming and managing a Business Hub profile is free at launch, per OpenAI. Any future paid features would be separate from claiming.

Can I claim without a website?

A website makes verification easier and gives ChatGPT more to read, but some businesses can verify by phone or email. For a travel agency, a site you own is worth having regardless.

How long does verification take?

Phone and email are quick. Domain (DNS) verification can take longer to propagate. The identity check is usually fast once submitted.

Does claiming improve my ranking in ChatGPT?

No. It fixes your facts and gives you data you can.t get elsewhere. Recommendations still come from your content, reviews and listings.

Someone else claimed my agency. What now?

Use the Hub's dispute path to prove you're the rightful owner. This is exactly why claiming early is worth it.
