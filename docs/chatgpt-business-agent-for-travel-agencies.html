ChatGPT business agents for travel agencies

By Latomic7 min readFor travel agencies & tour operators

A business agent is an assistant you publish inside ChatGPT that answers travelers' questions about your trips and policies. Here's who can use one, what it does, and whether it's worth it for your agency yet.

Short answer

A ChatGPT business agent answers shopper questions using details you supply. At launch it's limited to eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses, it's free, and it can't take payments, make bookings or check orders. For most travel agencies it's one to get ready for, not to wait on.

ChatGPT · a business agent in action
"Does your Kenya safari include park fees and domestic flights?"

Your published agent answers from the details you gave it: "Our 7-day Kenya safari includes park fees, game drives and domestic flights between camps. International flights aren't included." Then it links to the trip page to book.

How a business agent works

You publish an agent with your trips, inclusions and policies. When a shopper asks about them inside ChatGPT, the agent answers from what you supplied and points them to your site. It's a front desk inside ChatGPT, not a booking engine.

They're complementary. A website chatbot captures and books the travelers already on your site; a ChatGPT agent reaches travelers before they get there. Most agencies want the website one working first.

Getting ready

Whether or not you're eligible yet, the prep is the same: clear trip pages with inclusions and policies in plain text, a claimed profile, and consistent details. Do that and you can publish an agent the day you qualify, with nothing to scramble for.

Frequently asked questions

Is a ChatGPT business agent free?

It's free at launch. Any sponsored or paid features are separate.

Can a local travel shop create one?

Not at launch. Agents start with eligible US online businesses. A local agency can still claim a profile.

Can the agent take payments or bookings?

No. It answers questions and links to your site, where the booking happens.

Do I need Shopify?

Shopify is one path to eligibility and product data, but not the only one for online businesses.

Will I see travelers' chats with the agent?

Only in a limited way. You won't see every conversation.

How is it different from a website chatbot?

The agent lives in ChatGPT and can't book or charge; a site chatbot lives on your site and can. They work well together.
