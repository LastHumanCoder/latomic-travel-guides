Why isn't my travel agency showing up in ChatGPT?

By Latomic9 min readFor travel agencies & tour operators

A traveler asks ChatGPT for the best agency for their trip, and it names a competitor instead of you. Annoying, but usually fixable. It is nearly always one of seven causes, and you can check each in an afternoon.

Key things to know
  • 1If AI crawlers can't read your site, you can't be quoted. This is the most common and most missed cause.
  • 2ChatGPT leans on third-party reviews and directories, more than your own site. In travel that means TripAdvisor and Google as much as your pages.
  • 3Claiming a profile fixes your facts; it does not, by itself, make you appear more.
  • 4Answers vary by wording, city and sign-in, so one empty answer is not proof you're invisible.
  • 5Work the list in order. Most agencies are losing on one or two fixable points, not all seven.

Seven reasons ChatGPT skips your agency

Trip planning moved to AI assistants fast. Phocuswright found that 56% of travelers used AI for at least one trip in the past year, with generative tools like ChatGPT and Gemini reaching 33% for trip research. So when you are missing from those answers, you are missing from a real and growing shortlist. Here is where agencies usually fall out, strongest cause first.

Don't diagnose from one prompt. Run each question several times, on different days and devices. Assistants phrase answers differently every time, and a single miss is not a verdict.

Check that AI crawlers can read you

OpenAI uses separate crawlers for different jobs, and only its search crawler affects whether you can appear in answers. If your site quietly blocks it, nothing else on this list matters, because there is nothing for ChatGPT to quote.

Nofix robots.txt & firewall first

robots.txt

Open yoursite.com/robots.txt in a browser. If you see rules disallowing OAI-SearchBot (or a blanket Disallow: /), that is your problem. Allow the search crawler explicitly, and keep your real pages reachable.

Security plugins and firewalls

Many agency sites sit behind a security plugin or a CDN that blocks unfamiliar bots by default. That protection can turn away the exact crawler you want to let in. Check your firewall and bot-management settings, and allow the AI search crawlers through.

You're thin on reviews travelers trust

Travel is a trust purchase, and the assistants know it. In an independent test of 2,880 prompts across US metros, TripAdvisor appeared in 28.9% of ChatGPT's business answers and Yelp in the vast majority, while a direct pull from Google was rare. For a travel agency, that means your reviews on the big travel and local platforms do a lot of the talking. If the agencies ChatGPT names have hundreds of recent reviews and you have a handful, that gap alone can explain the silence.

Ask every happy traveler for a review while the trip is still fresh in their mind, and keep your profiles active. Steady, genuine reviews beat a one-off push.

ChatGPT has the wrong details

Sometimes you do appear, but with a wrong phone number, an old office address, or trips you no longer run. Fix the source first, then every copy. Conflicting details across your listings make the assistant unsure, and uncertainty is enough to send the recommendation elsewhere.

DetailFix it first onThen update
Name & categoryYour website & Google profileTripAdvisor, directories, socials
Phone & emailYour website contact pageEvery listing that shows contact details
Trips & destinationsYour own destination pagesAny directory that lists your specialities

It recommends competitors instead

You can't tell ChatGPT to stop naming a rival. What you can do is study why they win and close the gap. Look at what the named agencies have that you don't: more reviews, clearer destination pages, listings on more directories, prices stated plainly. Usually it is not one big thing; it is three small ones.

When it isn't you

Assistants are not consistent. The same question can name five different agencies across a week, and signing in or changing city changes the answer again. Before you conclude you're invisible, run each prompt ten or more times over several days, note the city and whether you were signed in, and look at the pattern rather than any single reply.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my travel agency showing up in ChatGPT?

Usually one of seven causes: your site isn't crawlable, nothing reputable cites you, you have no claimed profile, your details conflict across listings, your pages don't describe what you do, you're thin on reviews, or you're in the wrong category. Work the list in order.

Does blocking GPTBot remove me from ChatGPT search?

GPTBot is the training crawler, not the search crawler. The one that affects whether you can appear in answers is the search crawler (OAI-SearchBot). Check your robots.txt and firewall allow it.

Do reviews really decide this?

In travel, they carry real weight. ChatGPT leans heavily on review platforms, and TripAdvisor shows up often in its travel answers. If the named agencies have far more reviews than you, that gap alone can explain it.

Will claiming my business make me show up?

Claiming fixes your facts and gives you mention data, but it does not buy placement. Do it, then earn the signals that get you named.

How long do robots.txt changes take to matter?

There is no fixed timeline. Crawlers revisit over days to weeks. Make the change, keep your pages reachable, and re-check your visibility monthly.
