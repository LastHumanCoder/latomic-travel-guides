ChatGPT vs Google Business Profile for travel agencies

By Latomic8 min readFor travel agencies & tour operators

A ChatGPT profile is new; Google Business Profile and TripAdvisor are where travelers and assistants already look. Here's how they compare, where ChatGPT gets its answers, and which to claim first.

Short answer

They're not either/or. Google Business Profile still drives the most discovery and feeds the wider web. TripAdvisor and reviews do a lot of the talking in ChatGPT's travel answers. A ChatGPT profile lets you fix your facts and read mention data. Claim Google first, keep reviews strong, then claim ChatGPT.

Where ChatGPT gets its travel answers

ChatGPT doesn't simply read your Google profile. Independent testing of its business answers found it leans heavily on review platforms, with Yelp appearing most and, crucially for travel, TripAdvisor second, while a direct pull from Google was rare.

A note on the "Foursquare 60–70%" claim. Some guides say Foursquare supplies most of ChatGPT's local data. We couldn't verify that figure, and the testing above found Foursquare at 0%. Treat unconfirmed source claims with caution.

Do you need all three?

For most agencies, yes, because they do different jobs. Google is still where most travelers start and where the open web gets much of its data. TripAdvisor and reviews build the trust ChatGPT reads. The ChatGPT profile keeps your facts straight in the newest channel. The work overlaps, so keeping your name, phone, address and category identical across all three is most of the battle.

Frequently asked questions

Is ChatGPT replacing Google Business Profile?

No. Google still drives most discovery and feeds the wider web. ChatGPT is a new, additional channel.

Does ChatGPT use Google Business Profile?

Not directly, or only rarely in testing. It leans more on review platforms and the open web than on a direct Google pull.

Does ChatGPT use TripAdvisor?

TripAdvisor appeared often in tests of ChatGPT's business answers, making it an important travel signal.

Which directories does ChatGPT use?

Testing points to review platforms like Yelp and TripAdvisor more than map or social listings, though the mix shifts over time.

Which shows more data about my agency?

Google's insights are the richest today. ChatGPT's Business Hub shows mentions and clicks you can't get elsewhere.

Can I claim a ChatGPT profile outside the US?

It's rolling out by market. Check Business Hub for your country.
