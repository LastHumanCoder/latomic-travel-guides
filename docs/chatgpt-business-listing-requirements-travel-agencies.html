ChatGPT business listing requirements for travel agencies

By Latomic7 min readFor travel agencies & tour operators

Before you claim your agency in ChatGPT, it helps to know who qualifies, how verification works, and exactly what to have ready. Here's the checklist, with no guesswork.

Short answer

You can claim if you own or represent the agency, operate where ChatGPT supports verification, and either have an existing listing (local agencies) or can add one (many online agencies). You verify by phone, email, website domain or Shopify, then pass a Persona identity check. Have your registered name, website, phone, email and ID ready.

Who can claim a travel agency listing

  • You own the agency or are authorised to represent it.
  • You operate in a market where ChatGPT is available and OpenAI supports verification.
  • Local agencies and travel shops can claim an existing listing.
  • Online agencies may be able to add a new profile if none exists yet.

How long verification takes

Phone and email are quick. Domain verification depends on how fast your DNS TXT record propagates, which can take hours. The identity check is usually fast once submitted. Add the record, then wait before re-checking rather than redoing it.

Pending, failed or not eligible

If a claim sits pending, give it time; if it's rejected, re-check that your details match your listing and your ID matches your business records. If your agency isn't eligible yet, don't stall: the groundwork that earns recommendations (a readable site, reviews, consistent listings) works regardless, and makes you claim-ready the moment access opens.

Frequently asked questions

Who can claim a business in ChatGPT?

The owner or an authorised representative, in a supported market. Local agencies claim an existing listing; some online agencies can add one.

Do I need a website to claim?

It's not always required, but a site you own makes verification easier and gives ChatGPT more to read. For a travel agency it's worth having.

Can I claim without Shopify?

Yes. Shopify is one of four methods. Phone, email and domain verification don't need it.

How long does verification take?

Phone and email are quick; domain (DNS) can take hours; the identity check is usually fast.

What if someone already claimed my agency?

Use the dispute path in the Hub and prove ownership. Claiming early avoids this.
