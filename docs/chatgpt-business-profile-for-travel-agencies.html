Frequently asked questions

What is a ChatGPT business profile?

Your agency's verified entry inside ChatGPT, where you set your name, category, description, website, policies and trips.

Do I need one?

If travelers use ChatGPT to plan trips, yes, so your facts are right when you're mentioned. It's one part of being found by AI, alongside your website and reviews.

Is the profile free?

Claiming is free at launch, per OpenAI. Latomic's setup is included in your plan.

Is it like Google Business Profile?

Similar idea, different platform. See our ChatGPT vs Google Business Profile guide.

Can Latomic claim it without me?

We handle the work, but you authorise the claim and the identity check stays with you.

Will claiming make ChatGPT recommend me more?

Not by itself. It fixes your facts; recommendations come from your site, reviews and listings, which we also handle.
