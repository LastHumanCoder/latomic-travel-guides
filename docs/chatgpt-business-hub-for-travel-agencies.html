ChatGPT Business Hub for travel agencies

By Latomic7 min readFor travel agencies & tour operators

Business Hub is OpenAI's free dashboard where a travel agency claims its profile, edits its details, and sees how often ChatGPT mentions it. Here's what's inside and what it can and can't do.

Short answer

Business Hub is where you claim and manage your agency inside ChatGPT. It opens in stages: first a claim, then an identity check, then a complete profile and insights. It's free at launch. It shows mentions, website clicks and the topics travelers ask about, but it does not let you buy a higher ranking.

What opens, and when

Each stage opens the nextYou won't see insights until you've claimed and verified

Is Business Hub free?

Claiming, managing your profile and viewing insights are free at launch. OpenAI has signalled that some future features could carry a cost, and any ads or sponsored placements are separate from a free listing. For now, a travel agency pays nothing to claim and manage its profile.

What you can edit

Your name and category, description, logo and cover photo, website, destinations and trip types, and your delivery or booking policies. If you sell fixed packages, you may be able to connect a product feed so prices and availability stay current.

Reading the insights

MetricWhat it countsWhat it doesn't tell you
MentionsHow often ChatGPT referenced your agencyWho asked, or whether they booked
Website clicksClicks from ChatGPT to your siteWhat happened after the click
Discovery topicsThe themes travelers ask about near youExact wording of every question

Insights are a rolling window, not a live feed. Pair them with your own site analytics, and use the discovery topics as a to-do list of pages to write next. If "family tours" keeps appearing, that's your next page.

Team and agency access

You can add team members, and give an agency like Latomic access to manage the profile and reporting on your behalf. Agencies with several brands or offices can manage them from the same place.

What Business Hub can't do

It won't raise your ranking, book travelers, or tell you who asked about you. It's an accuracy-and-insight tool, not a sales channel. The recommendations still come from your website, reviews and listings.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get into ChatGPT Business Hub?

Sign in and open Business Hub from your account, then search for and claim your agency.

Is Business Hub free?

Claiming, managing and insights are free at launch. Some future features may cost; ads are separate.

Why are my insights empty?

Insights appear after you've claimed and verified, and once there's data in the window. A brand-new profile starts blank.

What counts as a mention?

A reference to your agency in a ChatGPT answer. It doesn't tell you who asked or whether they booked.

Can I give my agency access?

Yes. You can add team members or grant an agency access to manage and report.

How long do edits take to show?

There's no fixed time. Give changes a little while, and verify your public profile reflects them.
